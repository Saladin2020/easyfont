## Sample text

- The Thai sample is the traditional pangram poem (เป็นมนุษย์สุดประเสริฐเลิศคุณค่า…), public domain, plus hard stacking
  clusters written for the checks. Malay and English lines are written for Easy Suite.
- Demo dialogue features ป้านูรุล (Auntie Nurul), a tea-stall owner from the Easy Suite village.
